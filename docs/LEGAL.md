# Taiwan publication scope

Checked 15 October 2026 against Taiwan's official legislation database. These are limited publication choices, not a legal opinion or guarantee.

* [Copyright Act](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?PCode=J0070017), including Articles 10-1 and 11: distinguish ideas from protected expression and account for employment ownership. This repository contains original educational code and brief attributed analysis, not employer software, a copied advert or copied paper text. Dependencies retain their own licences.
* [Trade Secrets Act](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?pcode=J0080028): no confidential sensor specifications, tuning tables, employer datasets, internal algorithms or documents are included.
* [Personal Data Protection Act](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?PCode=I0050021): no personal images or personal records are published. Synthetic fixtures are generated locally. A later real-camera dataset requires a lawful collection/use/disclosure basis, purpose limits and an applicable transfer review. The official page marks some amendments as not yet effective; do not treat every displayed amendment as currently effective.
* [Patent Act](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?PCode=J0070007): this project makes no patentability or freedom-to-operate claim. A patentability and prior-art assessment of a specific invention is not possible from this advert. Publication can affect patent strategy.

The upstream SUPIR licence requires a separate commercial agreement, and PromptIR excludes commercial use. Their code and weights are not included. Other research repositories, their dependency models, weights and datasets require their own scope-specific licence checks before adoption. A repository-level licence does not automatically grant rights to every training image or checkpoint.

The original files are offered under the included MIT licence; this does not relicense third-party work or grant third-party patent rights.
